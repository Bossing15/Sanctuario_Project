# Gabay sa Contact Services

## 📞 CONTACT SERVICES OVERVIEW

The Contact Services module allows users to submit inquiries and messages to Sanctuario De Carmona Memorial Park. This is a public service available to everyone without login.

---

## 📋 CONTACT FORM FIELDS

### **Required Fields**

#### **1. First Name**
- **Type:** Text input
- **Placeholder:** "First name"
- **Validation:** 
  - Required (cannot be empty)
  - Minimum 2 characters
  - Maximum 50 characters
  - Letters and spaces only
- **Example:** "Juan"

#### **2. Last Name**
- **Type:** Text input
- **Placeholder:** "Last name"
- **Validation:**
  - Required (cannot be empty)
  - Minimum 2 characters
  - Maximum 50 characters
  - Letters and spaces only
- **Example:** "Dela Cruz"

#### **3. Email Address**
- **Type:** Email input
- **Placeholder:** "Your email"
- **Validation:**
  - Required (cannot be empty)
  - Must be valid email format
  - Example: user@example.com
  - Maximum 100 characters
- **Example:** "juan.delacruz@email.com"

#### **4. Contact Number (Phone)**
- **Type:** Telephone input
- **Placeholder:** "Your phone number"
- **Validation:**
  - Optional (can be empty)
  - Recommended format: +63 9XX XXX XXXX
  - Minimum 10 digits
  - Maximum 15 digits
  - Numbers and symbols (+, -, space) only
- **Example:** "+63 912 345 6789"

#### **5. Message**
- **Type:** Text area
- **Placeholder:** "Your message or question"
- **Validation:**
  - Required (cannot be empty)
  - Minimum 10 characters
  - Maximum 5000 characters
  - Can include letters, numbers, punctuation
- **Example:** "I would like to inquire about your grave cleaning services..."

## 📝 HOW TO SUBMIT A MESSAGE

### **Step 1: Fill in First Name**
1. Click on "First name" field
2. Type your first name
3. Example: "Maria"

### **Step 2: Fill in Last Name**
1. Click on "Last name" field
2. Type your last name
3. Example: "Santos"

### **Step 3: Fill in Email**
1. Click on "Your email" field
2. Type your email address
3. Example: "maria.santos@email.com"
4. Make sure email is correct (we'll use this to reply)

### **Step 4: Fill in Phone Number (Optional)**
1. Click on "Your phone number" field
2. Type your phone number
3. Format: +63 9XX XXX XXXX
4. Example: "+63 917 123 4567"

### **Step 5: Write Your Message**
1. Click on "Your message or question" field
2. Type your message
3. Be clear and specific about your inquiry
4. Minimum 10 characters required
5. Example: "I would like to know more about your grave maintenance services and pricing."

### **Step 6: Submit**
1. Click **"Submit"** button
2. Wait for confirmation message
3. You'll receive a success notification
